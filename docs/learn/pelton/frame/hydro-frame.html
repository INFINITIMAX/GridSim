Pelton micro-hydro

Water taken from a stream high up drops through a pipe, leaves a nozzle as a fast jet and spins a Pelton runner. The simplest way to turn falling water into electricity.

Needs a big fall, only a little water
No dam: a small weir diverts part of the stream
Runner and generator share one shaft
